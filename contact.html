Sanctuary & Safety Guidelines

1
Unaccompanied Sessions

To maintain medical-grade sterility and quiet concentration, procedure rooms are reserved strictly for the client and master artist.

2
48-Hour Pre-Care Notice

Avoid caffeine, aspirin/blood-thinners, alcohol, and active chemical retinols for 48 hours prior to minimize capillary bleeding and maximize pigment lock.

3
All-Inclusive 6-Week Touch-Up

All procedures include our clinical 6-week perfecting session to adjust density, check skin retention, and ensure perfection.
