The Atelier Methodology

Our Scientific & Artistic Approach

We reject one-size-fits-all stencils. Every line is hand-calculated to harmonise with facial muscle movement and bone morphology.

1. Golden Ratio 1.618 Mapping

Brows are mathematically measured against your ocular spacing and nasal bridge using precision calipers. You review and approve the hand-drawn preview before any pigment is placed.

2. Organic Mineral Chemistry

We formulate with heavy-metal-free, hypoallergenic Swiss mineral pigments that do not turn reddish or grayish over time, fading evenly and naturally as your skin sheds.

3. 0.15mm Micro-Stylus

Depositing micro-pigment only into the basal layer (0.08mm - 0.15mm depth). This preserves skin tissue elasticity and ensures hair strokes stay crisp without pigment bleeding.

4. 6-Week Perfecting Session

Every primary procedure includes a complimentary 6-week perfecting session to inspect cellular retention, reinforce lighter strokes, and calibrate the ultimate final tone.
