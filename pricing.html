Frequently Asked Questions

Essential clinical guidelines on comfort, healing timelines, bridal preparation, and contraindications.

Topical medical-grade numbing ointment goes on 20 minutes before and continuously during the session. Most clients describe brow feathering as a light, rhythmic scratching sensation. Lips are the most sensitive of the facial regions, but nobody has ever asked to stop.
Microblading typically lasts 12 to 18 months, nanoblading 18 to 24 months, and powder brows / lip blush up to 3 years. Oily skin, sun exposure, swimming, and active exfoliating skincare (Retinoids, AHA/BHA) will shorten longevity, which is why an annual refresh session is part of the maintenance plan.
Days 1–3: Brows and lips appear 30% bolder and darker than the final result.
Days 4–10: Superficial micro-flaking occurs. Never pick or scrub the skin.
Weeks 3–4: The "ghosting phase" occurs where pigment looks temporarily light while new skin cells form.
Week 6: The true rich tone resurfaces completely — exactly when the complimentary perfecting touch-up takes place.

Permanent makeup is contraindicated for anyone pregnant or nursing, individuals taking Accutane within the past 6 months, those on prescription blood thinners, active eczema/psoriasis in the treatment area, or individuals prone to keloid scarring. Diabetic and autoimmune conditions require a physician's written clearance.
