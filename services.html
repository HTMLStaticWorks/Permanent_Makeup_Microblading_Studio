The Science of Semi-Permanent Artistry

Every face has distinct bone geometry, skin elasticity, and undertones. We match the precise needle configuration and pigment chemistry to guarantee undetectable, natural longevity.

Best for Normal / Dry Skin
Treatment 01

Hyper-Realistic Microblading

Hand-carved hair strokes created using a microscopic multi-prong blade. Each individual stroke is calibrated in direction, curvature, and depth to blend seamlessly with existing hair follicles.

Ideal Candidate: Normal to dry skin with patchy or sparse brows
Longevity: 12 to 18 Months before gentle annual refresh
Pain Level: Minimal (1/10) with 2-step topical lidocaine
From ₹32,000
The Modern Gold Standard
Treatment 02

Digital Acoustic Nanoblading

Unlike traditional manual microblading, nanoblading utilizes an ultra-fine single acoustic needle driven by digital frequency. It deposits pigment into the upper dermis without cutting tissue, making it the supreme choice for oily, mature, or sensitive skin.

Ideal Candidate: Oily skin, large pores, or sensitive delicate skin
Longevity: 18 to 24 Months with zero stroke blurring
Trauma Level: Zero tissue cutting · Rapid 4-day healing
From ₹38,000
Velvet Gradient Look
Treatment 03

Ombré Powder & Combo Brows

A soft, pixelated misting technique that is translucent at the bulb and builds into rich, velvety density at the arch and tail. Replaces daily pencil, brow pomade, and setting gels completely.

Ideal Candidate: All skin types seeking structured makeup definition
Longevity: 2 to 3.5 Years
Retention Rate: 95%+ on South Asian skin
From ₹36,000
Specialized Melanin Neutralization
Treatment 04

Lip Blush & Melanin Neutralization

Dark lip neutralization requires specialized color theory. We first neutralize cool grey and hyperpigmented melanin with warm peach/terracotta bases, followed by a second session infused with your dream rose, nude, or berry wash.

Session Structure: 2 Sessions (Neutralize + Tint) included
Longevity: 2 to 3 Years
Effect: Fuller illusion, balanced border & natural flush
